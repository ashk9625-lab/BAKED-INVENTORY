BAKED INVENTORY – INSTALL APP UPGRADE

Upload these files into your existing:
BAKED-BAKING-INVENTORY-V2

IMPORTANT:
1. Keep the exact folder structure.
2. REPLACE:
   - app/components.js
   - app/layout.js
3. ADD:
   - app/install-button.js
   - public/manifest.webmanifest
   - public/service-worker.js
   - public/icon-192.png
   - public/icon-512.png
   - public/apple-touch-icon.png
4. COPY the contents of app/globals-PWA-ADD.css to the BOTTOM of your existing app/globals.css.
   Do NOT replace your whole globals.css with this small file.

After upload, Vercel should deploy automatically.

HOW STAFF INSTALL IT

ANDROID:
- Open https://baked-inventory.vercel.app in Chrome.
- Tap “Install App” in the left menu.
- Confirm Install.
- The BAKED Inventory app appears on the phone home screen/app drawer.

IPHONE / IPAD:
- Open https://baked-inventory.vercel.app in Safari.
- Tap “Install App”.
- Follow the message: Share → Add to Home Screen.
- Tap Add.

DESKTOP:
- Chrome/Edge can also install the inventory as an app.

NOTE:
The app still uses the live online database. The service worker is mainly for app-shell/install support.
